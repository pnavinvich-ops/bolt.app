Privacy Policy for ArmLog

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ArmLog ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

ArmLog is designed to track arm wrestling training routines and workouts. We collect minimal data necessary to provide app functionality:

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your email address for authentication.
  • Workout Data: Exercise logs, set counts, weights, and progress entries created within the app.
  • Device & Usage Data: Local storage settings and standard technical diagnostics to maintain performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to deliver, improve, and secure your ArmLog account and training logs.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely transmitted over HTTPS and stored using modern database protections. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You can request account deletion or data removal at any time by contacting our support team.

5. Contact Us

For support or privacy concerns, reach out to us at: support@bolt-app-psi.vercel.app
